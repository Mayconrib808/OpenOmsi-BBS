# Code of Conduct / Código de Conduta

## Our community

OpenOmsi + BBS welcomes players and contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels and languages. These rules apply to Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions and project-related support.

- Be respectful and patient. Help newcomers understand the problem.
- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Accept constructive feedback and explain disagreements with evidence.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or intimidate others.
- Do not publish another person's private information, account details or private messages without permission.
- Keep reports relevant to the project. Avoid spam, impersonation and misleading claims of official support.
- Respect the licenses of submitted code and content.

## Reporting and moderation

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to **Mayconrib808** on Discord **`.zmaycon.`** (both dots). Include the project link and a concise explanation. Do not publish private material in a public Issue.

The maintainer reviews reports, considers the context and may request clarification. Depending on severity and repeated behaviour, moderation may include a request to correct the behaviour, a warning, removal of inappropriate content, or a temporary or permanent restriction from project spaces. Reports are handled with discretion; private details are shared only when needed to address the report.

## Português do Brasil

Jogadores e colaboradores são bem-vindos independentemente de experiência, origem ou idioma. Respeite as pessoas, critique ideias sem ataques pessoais e aceite comentários construtivos. Assédio, ameaças, discriminação, spam e divulgação de dados privados não são aceitos.

As regras valem para Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions e suporte do projeto. Relate problemas de conduta por mensagem privada no Discord **`.zmaycon.`**. Conforme o caso, a moderação pode orientar, advertir, remover conteúdo ou restringir a participação.
