BBS+OpenOmsi Multiplayer — 3.0.2-dev.1
Development preview for private testing, not a public release.

1. Extract into a separate folder, keeping your working 2.x installation.
2. Open Configurar.exe, choose your language and save a company invitation or create a room.
3. Close BCS and click Configure BCS. Finish setup, then start the trip in BCS.

No BCS JAR or DLL is supplied or modified. Next trip and the 3.x update feed are still pending.
The update-files ZIP is only for an existing 3.x installation. Keep its config folder.
This independent project is not an official PeDePe product.
