## English

**BBS+OpenOmsi Multiplayer 3.0 — by Mayconrib808**

Drive your Bus Company Simulator / Busbetrieb-Simulator trips together in openOMSI multiplayer. BCS continues to choose and evaluate your company trip; openOMSI supplies the shared world, traffic and passengers. Drivers on the same map can encounter one another's buses.

Create a company invitation once and share it with your members. The first driver on each map starts a dedicated session automatically; later drivers on that map join it. Different maps, such as Berlin and Carrão City, use independent sessions and tunnels. The host PC must remain on and connected while peers are playing.

Requirements: Windows x64, your own OMSI 2 and **BCS 5.0.2.0**, the map/dependencies and your own bus. Client and server must use openOMSI **protocol 7**, introduced with **0.2.48**. The official 0.2.47 was not released. The package includes the unmodified official **0.2.50 server**. A compatible existing client, including a separately configured DLSS/ReShade installation, can be selected in Setup.

Extract the complete ZIP, close BCS and run Setup.exe. Choose Portuguese, English or German; create/join the company room; save and configure BCS. In BCS select **OpenOMSI (Beta)** and the package's **OpenOMSI-BBS/PeDePeAdapter** folder. Start trips normally from BCS. Finish through its native flow, then use **Next trip** with the game open. The original PeDePe Lua is forwarded unchanged and no BCS JAR patch is applied.

The source, offline guide, licenses and SHA-256 checksums are included. Updates use the separate verified Cloudflare 3.x channel. Solo mode, automatic/manual checks, deactivation and local log collection are available. Missing peer models may use stand-ins; install the same bus/repaint to see the exact model.

**Current download is a release candidate:** live two-PC trip completion and next-trip validation remain required before the final release. Antivirus evidence must refer to the exact published hashes; no universal zero-detection claim is made.

Eine Firmeneinladung verbindet die Mitglieder mit der passenden Kartensitzung. Der erste Fahrer jeder Karte startet den dedizierten Server automatisch. Berlin und Carrão City verwenden getrennte Sitzungen und Tunnel. Der Host-PC muss eingeschaltet und online bleiben.
