# OpenOMSI BCS Bridge 2.2.1.9

**Complete cumulative changelog — everything added or improved since 2.1.9 (through 2.2.1.9 Hotfix 2 + the BCS localization fix).**

The 2.2 series is a major step beyond the original BCS-to-openOMSI adapter. This download includes the modified cooperative openOMSI game, the Bridge, Setup, PeDePe adapter, company hosts, P14/P15 dedicated servers, required runtime files, source code and licensing notices.

## What's new since 2.1.9

### 1. Cooperative driving and conductor gameplay

- The modified **openOMSI Coop** game is included with the Bridge: players no longer need a separately prepared Coop package.
- Players can **hand over the steering wheel** to another connected player. The visiting driver receives vehicle controls, physics and scripts while the original owner retains the BCS trip/evaluation.
- The driver's former bus is hidden/suspended during handover and restored when control is returned. Driver and conductor permissions follow the current vehicle authority.
- Added cooperative **conductor/ticket collector** gameplay and improved the ticket dispenser, coins, banknotes, payments and change shared between players.
- The conductor can collect payments using **Shift+T**, the collect-money button or the mouse. Collection acknowledgments prevent the same payment from being counted twice.
- Improved bus interior interaction, seating, camera F1/F4, ticket selection and remote control acknowledgments. Fixed cases of duplicate/invisible buses, unresponsive controls, incorrect handover ownership and players appearing back where the driver switch started.
- Fixed lost passenger cabins and incorrect occupancy displays after control transfer. The original bus simulation's passenger count is retained in the HUD.
- Improved jitter, sound-event congestion, remote camera synchronization and overloaded player/passenger network queues. A live two-player test is still necessary for gameplay validation.

### 2. Multiplayer characters, buses and networking

- Added selectable `.hum` player characters/skins through **Alt → Cooperative**, with search, pages and persistence across sessions.
- Players can **start on foot**, be visible to others without owning a bus, and walk or sit inside a shared vehicle.
- Character appearance and bus livery are better synchronized when entering/exiting buses, changing roles or respawning.
- Added support for native bus/livery transfers with compatible openOMSI servers, while keeping local stand-in vehicles for older configurations. Maps and dependencies still need to be installed by each player.
- Improved dynamic world loading around players walking on foot and buses, plus reconnection and authenticated company-server discovery.
- Added separate, maintained **P14 and P15** server distributions. The current bundled Coop uses **openOMSI 0.3.45, protocol 15**. Players and servers must use matching protocols.

### 3. Passenger signals, gestures and route awareness

- Passengers can signal an approaching bus after recognizing an eligible line/destination, instead of signaling every vehicle from far away.
- Refined the signal range, approach direction, indicator finger, arm position, moving palm/wrist and head/body gaze.
- Prevented unnatural neck twisting, fingers pointing backward and repeated gestures at buses with no room to board.
- Improved walking/feet positioning around stops, visual synchronization and heavy passenger-spawn performance.
- Passengers already waiting in heavy rain or entering a rainy scene can display umbrella behavior without requiring weather to change again.

### 4. Rain, shelters and umbrella effects

- Added umbrellas opening during rain, folding when boarding, and reopening when needed.
- Supported overhead cover checks so umbrellas can close under recognized roofs or shelters.
- Added visible raindrops, water flow and edge dripping to wet umbrellas.
- Improved umbrella rotation/folding and fixed water effects disappearing prematurely when an umbrella begins closing.
- Moved some weather/sky preparation off the main rendering path to reduce certain stalls; this does not eliminate all map/addon frame drops.

### 5. Connecting passengers and ticket-purchase controls

- Added an **experimental route-transfer system**: eligible passengers can alight, walk to another nearby stop, wait for a different route and board a connecting service.
- Also supports eligible transfers **at the same stop** and avoids reboarding/signaling the bus that was just left.
- Added a configurable transfer probability **0–100%, default 20%**. This is a simulation setting, not a universal policy forced on every dedicated-server client.
- Improved return of connecting passengers to the authoritative multiplayer simulation in later 2.2.1 builds.
- Added an optional **ticket-purchase probability slider (0–100%)**, disabled by default, with support for eligible new boardings when a ticket-selling device is installed.
- Improved ticket labels/prices and physical payments used by both driver and conductor.

### 6. BCS integration, next trips and fuel

- Improved **Next Trip** detection, timetable continuation, native dispatch and destination/IBIS index checking. A destination correction is acknowledged only after the resulting bus display is verified.
- Added support for newer inspected BCS builds, including BCS **5.0.3.0**, and removed the rigid whole-JAR version/hash gate.
- Native BCS multiplayer choices are respected instead of accidentally layering company joining over an existing native session.
- Introduced the option to **start with the bus engine switched off** while preserving timetable, vehicle, starting position, fuel and damage data.
- Improved external BCS **fuel priority**, including zero fuel, rather than resetting all buses to a full tank. Fixed the invalid `--keep-fuel` launch argument.
- Improved BCS trip/evaluation continuity when drivers change during cooperative play.

### 7. Servers, administration and AI

- Added server/map **status indicators** in Setup, manual refresh, and a list of active map sessions with copyable player join addresses.
- Improved HostAgent/CompanyHost compatibility and recovery during slow map loads, including temporary directory/network failures and clock synchronization.
- Added optional authenticated **exact server time (HH:MM:SS)** and speeds up to **32×**, after initial company clock synchronization. This option is off by default and requires administrator privileges.
- Preserved the complete P15 host controls (voice/radio settings, public listing, identification rules, map administration and passwords).
- Improved AI greetings with **two horn taps**, envelope smoothing, timing, distance, cabin filtering and bounded network audio events.
- Preserved the custom door-closing sound and other established gameplay assets.

### 8. Cameras, Setup and updates

- Added a floating **Alt → Camera** cab editor for live changes to seat position, height, view direction, field of view and mirrors, including per-bus save/reset options.
- Added Setup options for **backup/restore of supported modified Coop files**, with verification and preservation of user settings.
- Improved update recovery for incomplete installations, Setup-to-updater handoff and background server-status refresh.
- Added/retained GUI translations in **Portuguese, English and German**.
- Fixed **Update Now** rejecting an unsaved OMSI 2 folder entered in Setup.
- **BCS localization fix:** the PeDePe adapter accepts both `Bus Company Simulator` and `Simulador de Companhia de Autobuses` as the saved driver identifier without altering the driver's original data. Other localized identifier strings have not been validated.

## Installation and updating

1. Download **`OpenOMSI_BCS_Bridge_2.2.1.9.zip`** from the **Assets** section below (not GitHub's autogenerated source archive).
2. Extract the **whole ZIP** to a permanent folder **outside the OMSI 2 game folder**.
3. Close BCS, OMSI 2, openOMSI and the Bridge/HostAgent processes.
4. Open `Setup.exe`, select your OMSI 2 installation and the bundled openOMSI Coop executable, then save/activate.
5. In BCS, choose **OpenOMSI (Beta)** and point to the included `PeDePeAdapter/openomsi.exe`.
6. When upgrading, back up your existing Bridge installation and preserve any personal `app/bridge.ini`, company settings, invitations, custom maps and host data.

**This is a full cumulative package**; you do not need to install each intermediate 2.1.9/2.2.x version separately. For cooperative sessions, update all players and the P15 company host to compatible builds and start a new session.

## Compatibility and testing

- Requires **Windows, a legitimate OMSI 2 installation and Bus Company Simulator**. Commercial games, paid addons and maps are **not included**.
- The current bundled modified openOMSI is based on **0.3.45 Coop15 / protocol 15**. Legacy P14 remains separate; clients and servers cannot mix incompatible protocols.
- The core Windows binaries and focused automated tests passed. However, **a full real two-PC BCS trip with this exact localized distribution has not been verified**, and some multiplayer/AI/animation behavior can vary with installed maps and vehicles.
- This is an **experimental community project**. For issues, include relevant logs and the affected game/BCS versions.
