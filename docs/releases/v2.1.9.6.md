# OpenOMSI BCS Bridge 2.1.9.6

## Português

Corrige o pedido de transferência de ônibus introduzido no openOMSI 0.3.36 e mantido na 0.3.37. O canal da empresa responde, no protocolo nativo, que não aceita uploads. O jogo pode reconhecer essa condição e encerrar a tentativa normalmente, sem tratar o compartilhamento desativado como uma conexão perdida (erro 10054).

O canal de arquivos fica separado das conexões dos jogadores: não reserva uma vaga, não encaminha arquivos ao servidor e não expõe sua administração. A configuração temporária também define `accept_uploads = 0` e `share_maps = 0`. O arquivo `server.cfg` original é preservado. O uso de ônibus privados e os substitutos locais continuam disponíveis.

Atualização cumulativa da linha 2.1, incluindo as correções da 2.1.9.5. Mantém os protocolos 6, 7 e 14; jogadores e servidor precisam usar protocolos compatíveis. Não habilita o download automático de ônibus entre jogadores nem substitui o openOMSI instalado.

Instalação: feche BCS, openOMSI, Setup, HostAgent e CompanyHost; execute `Atualizar-Bridge.exe`; reabra o HostAgent no computador do host e depois o BCS. Empresa, convite, pastas e preferências são preservados. A correção desse canal precisa estar instalada no host da empresa.

## English

Handles the bus-upload request introduced by openOMSI 0.3.36 and retained in 0.3.37. The company gateway answers the native mod-stream greeting with the recognised "this server takes no uploads" refusal, allowing the uploader to stop normally instead of reporting a lost connection (Windows error 10054).

File requests do not allocate a gameplay slot, forward files to the server, or expose its administration. The private runtime configuration explicitly disables uploads and map sharing; the original `server.cfg` is preserved. Private buses and local stand-ins remain available.

Cumulative 2.1 update including 2.1.9.5. Protocols 6, 7 and 14 remain supported; clients and server need compatible protocols. Automatic bus downloads are not enabled and installed game/server binaries are not replaced. Close BCS, openOMSI, Setup, HostAgent and CompanyHost, run `Atualizar-Bridge.exe`, then reopen HostAgent on the host and BCS. The company host needs this update to fix its file channel. Company, invitation, paths and preferences are preserved.

## Deutsch

Behandelt die Bus-Upload-Anfrage aus openOMSI 0.3.36, die auch in 0.3.37 verwendet wird. Das Firmen-Gateway beantwortet die native Begrüßung des Dateikanals mit der erkannten Ablehnung "this server takes no uploads". Dadurch kann der Upload normal beendet werden, statt einen Verbindungsabbruch zu melden (Windows-Fehler 10054).

Dateianfragen belegen keinen Spielerplatz, leiten keine Dateien an den Server weiter und geben dessen Administration nicht frei. Die temporäre Serverkonfiguration deaktiviert Uploads und Kartenfreigabe ausdrücklich; die ursprüngliche `server.cfg` bleibt erhalten. Private Busse und lokale Ersatzmodelle können weiterhin verwendet werden.

Kumulatives Update der Reihe 2.1 einschließlich 2.1.9.5. Die Protokolle 6, 7 und 14 bleiben unterstützt; Spieler und Server benötigen kompatible Protokolle. Automatische Bus-Downloads werden nicht aktiviert; installierte Spiel-/Serverdateien bleiben erhalten. BBS, openOMSI, Setup, HostAgent und CompanyHost schließen, `Atualizar-Bridge.exe` ausführen, danach HostAgent auf dem Host und BBS wieder öffnen. Der Firmen-Host benötigt dieses Update für die Korrektur des Dateikanals. Firma, Einladung, Pfade und Einstellungen bleiben erhalten.

## Validation

Regression coverage includes the exact native refusal, TCP chunking, WebSocket fragmentation and ping, normal close, invalid/oversized requests, no gameplay identity/slot, private configuration, protocols 6/7/14 and a normal player connection after refusal. The official 0.3.37 server is pinned by the published Windows/Linux archive SHA-256 for integration checks. Automated validation does not replace a complete trip in the installed Windows game and BCS.
