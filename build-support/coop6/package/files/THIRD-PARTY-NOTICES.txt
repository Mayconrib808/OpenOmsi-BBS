This is an unofficial experimental derivative of openOMSI v0.3.38, cooperative revision coop.4.1.
Upstream: https://github.com/openOMSI-org/openOMSI
Base commit: 866c60de268f3fc8737287b8e2d13993c49b1f1f
License: AGPL-3.0-only. Corresponding modified source and build notes are in source/.

The support programs and DLLs in programa/ are unmodified files from the official
openOMSI-0.3.38-windows-x64.zip, except programa/openomsi.exe (cooperative build) and steam_api64.dll
(the unmodified matching steamworks-sys redistributable described below). The MinGW runtime DLLs described below were added.
The official archive's LICENSE, LICENSE-ANGLE.txt and README.md are preserved in
programa/. The release archive and individual file digests are in PACKAGE-INFO.json.
Official release: https://github.com/openOMSI-org/openOMSI/releases/tag/v0.3.38

The official support programs openomsi-launcher.exe and openomsi-launcher-app.exe
are distributed unchanged; the included complete source tree contains their
corresponding source at the pinned upstream tag. The portable entry point
ABRIR-COOPERATIVO.exe is built from source/portable-launcher/start-coop.c and the
resources in that directory, under AGPL-3.0-only. It is not an upstream release.

steam_api64.dll is an unmodified Valve Steam API redistributable taken from
Noxime/steamworks-rs tag v0.13.0, steamworks-sys/lib/steam/redistributable_bin/
win64/steam_api64.dll, matching the pinned steamworks-sys 0.13.0 dependency.
Valve retains ownership and its applicable redistribution terms; the Rust
binding's MIT/Apache licenses do not apply to the Steam SDK files. Provenance
and exact hashes are recorded in source/STEAM-RUNTIME-PROVENANCE.json.
Preserve upstream's asset, Steam and dependency notices in the source archive.
An original OMSI 2 installation remains required. No commercial maps, vehicles
or other proprietary OMSI game content are supplied by this package.

libEGL.dll and libGLESv2.dll are the upstream release's unmodified ANGLE runtime.
Its included redistribution notice is preserved as programa/LICENSE-ANGLE.txt.
dxcompiler.dll, dxil.dll, msvcp140.dll, vcruntime140.dll and vcruntime140_1.dll are
also preserved unchanged from that official Windows release. Their ownership
and redistribution terms remain those of their respective upstream suppliers.

The GNU Windows runtime files libstdc++-6.dll, libgcc_s_seh-1.dll and
libwinpthread-1.dll come from the Ubuntu-distributed MinGW-w64 GCC 13 toolchain.
Their package copyright/license documents are provided in licenses/, including
the GCC Runtime Library Exception where applicable. Only debugging information
was removed from those runtime DLLs. The Steam redistributable was not modified.
Source projects: https://gcc.gnu.org/ and https://www.mingw-w64.org/.

Rust dependencies retain the versions pinned by the included Cargo.lock. Their
source repositories and license declarations remain those of upstream.
